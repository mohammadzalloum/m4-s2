# Comparative Analysis

### Your KPI and chart
Mohammad chose the KPI **Revenue by City and Category** because it makes profit patterns easier to explain and shows where revenue is strongest across cities and categories. He used a **heatmap** because it allows fast comparison in one view, and the colors were chosen to stay readable and colorblind-friendly.

### Your partner’s KPI and chart
Osama chose the KPI **Monthly Revenue** to track profit over time and see whether there is a clear pattern or trend. He used a **line chart** because the data is time-based, and a line chart is the clearest way to show changes across months.

### Where you agreed
We agreed that both charts help explain the business clearly and make patterns easier to understand. Mohammad’s chart shows **where** revenue is strongest, while Osama’s chart shows **when** revenue rises or slows down. Together, they give a more complete view of the market.

### Where you disagreed
Our main difference was in focus. Mohammad focused on geographic and category performance, while Osama focused on time trends. We also used different chart types because each KPI answers a different business question.

### What you learned
We learned that one chart alone does not show the full story. Combining category, city, and time-based analysis gives a stronger understanding of market performance.
